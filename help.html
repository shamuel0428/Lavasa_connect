Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Will my complaint be sent to the utility provider?

No. This is a front-end prototype and saves reports only in the current browser. Real submissions need a secure backend and a verified recipient workflow.

Why are local helpline numbers not listed?

Unverified numbers could misdirect residents. Confirm the responsible local electricity and water operators and their official contact numbers before publishing.

Why are live outage updates missing?

The site has no official data feed connected. An authorised team needs to enter verified notices through an admin system or integrate a provider API.

Is it safe to pay through the website?

The website only launches a UPI app or opens a bank website. It does not process payments. Verify the payee, biller and amount before confirming a transaction.
